# Dummy 2.5" SSD

Fills the unused 2.5" drive slot of the REDUX case when only one SSD is installed, so the
empty bracket does not rattle.

- Standard 2.5" 7 mm envelope (SFF-8201): 100 × 69.85 × 7 mm.
- Mounting holes underneath and on the sides at 14 and 90.6 mm from the connector end,
  sized as 2.6 mm pilot holes for M3 screws (they self-tap into the plastic).
- Hollow with ribs: quick to print, no supports.
